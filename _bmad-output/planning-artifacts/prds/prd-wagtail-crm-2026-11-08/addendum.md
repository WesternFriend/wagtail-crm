- Use JSContact for supported contact semantics; evaluate the exact profile against [RFC 9553](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9553.html) as the canonical common-contact baseline, including full name, optional typed components and ordering. This replaces Schema.org as the proposed primary contact model. Adoption of the standards division does not yet establish the supported properties, validation rules or extensions.
- Use [CLDR Person Names](https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr35/tr35-personNames.html) for generated name formatting, separately from storage semantics. Pin the supported published version during profile design; missing structure remains unknown. Formatting cannot establish missing name structure or override confirmed correspondence preferences.
- Define bounded interoperability mappings to [Schema.org Person](https://schema.org/Person), [Organization](https://schema.org/Organization), [PostalAddress](https://schema.org/PostalAddress) and [ContactPoint](https://schema.org/ContactPoint), and [vCard 4.0, RFC 6350](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6350.html). Include [RFC 9554](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9554.html) extensions and [RFC 9555](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9555.html) conversion rules when defining the vCard mapping; do not promise full vCard support or lossless conversions outside the chosen profile.
- Evaluate [ISO 4217](https://www.iso.org/iso-4217-currency-codes.html) for explicit currency codes with decimal amounts, and [RFC 3339](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3339.html) for exported instants. Neither expands initial USD calculations nor determines reporting-date policy.
