# Wagtail CRM — product brief

Status: approved by Brylie as product direction, 2026-11-08. This approval does not confirm stakeholder authority, financial policies, or operational readiness; those require separate confirmation and evidence.

## Purpose and north star

Give nonprofits an affordable, maintainable Apache-licensed CRM they can run and adapt, with control over hosting and private, portable data. Make operational and financial decisions understandable and traceable so staff successors can continue the work.

Western Friend's Editor needs annual donor acknowledgments from PayPal and related donor/subscription evidence. The first product proof is a repeatable, reviewable cycle that staff can own. Over time, success means independent organizations in multiple countries running their workflows, maintaining installations, and contributing improvements. Expand capabilities through actual adoption; avoid organization-specific forks for supported work.

PayPal is the first supported source. Core donor and contribution records remain processor-neutral so other sources can build on the same foundation as needs emerge.

## First useful release

One explicitly agreed acknowledgment cycle, covering every agreed source and account: ingest records and donor context, resolve identities and duplicates, review charitable allocations and refunds, confirm coverage, reconcile, approve exact letters, issue PDFs, distribute through agreed tools, and record dispatch. Retain evidence, reasons, approvals, and immutable prior issuance through corrections.

English interface and standard letters; USD calculations/reporting. International donor names and correspondence remain supported. Unsupported currency and language needs stay visible and require explicit handling. Offline gift capture is essential if included in agreed coverage; choose CSV or controlled entry after inspecting records. Contact import necessity depends on volume.

## People and value

The Editor operates the interface after brief onboarding. Finance confirms financial basis and material exceptions. A technical maintainer provides occasional installation, update, and recovery support. Exact authority and named operational owners remain unconfirmed.

Success means explainable contribution amounts, no unexplained reconciliation differences, no duplicate effects, preserved letter history, private records, and useful exports. The Editor must distinguish preparation, approval, issuance, and dispatch, recognize exceptions, and seek finance help appropriately without needing a developer for routine work.

Routine work uses plain language, minimizes repeated work, and shows clear next actions. Amounts, matching and allocation suggestions, and blocked actions have understandable explanations.

Affordability covers hosting, maintenance, training, support and staff time; no numeric target exists. Documented installation, predictable upgrades, tested recovery and handover are part of maintainability.

## Boundaries

Defer live synchronization, direct website integration, additional processors, application email, campaigns, portals, fulfillment, advanced analytics, shared multi-organization hosting and additional-country reporting rules. Use existing delivery tools and manual dispatch attestations initially, subject to confirmation.

Small exceptional cases may justify controlled manual handling; substantial repetition may justify another essential capability. A complete agreed pilot subset is useful but does not fulfill the wider annual obligation. Unverifiable identity, money, policy or coverage blocks affected operational use. Deadlines do not override trust controls.

Documented delivery exceptions may permit cycle closure under an agreed policy, while undelivered letters retain their status. Unresolved coverage, donor attribution, or financial approval continues to block affected issuance; delivery exceptions do not waive those requirements.

## Pilot and next evidence

Primary question: can Western Friend's staff independently own and understand the supported workflow? Test asynchronously with representative synthetic records on a restricted environment and the Editor's Mac desktop. Short tasks, brief feedback and inspected outcomes are primary evidence; calls and live observation supplement them.

Synthetic-data UX testing establishes usability and understanding. Financial validation separately compares results with stakeholder-approved source evidence and independently established expected results. Operational readiness requires both, together with the privacy, recovery, and ownership evidence defined in the supporting requirements and validation plan.
